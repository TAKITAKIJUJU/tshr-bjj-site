01 / Concept

What is Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu?

01

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is a sport in which you learn grappling without strikes such as punches or kicks. You can train at your own pace according to your age and exercise experience.

01 / SAFETY

Ground Grappling (No Strikes)

There are no strikes such as punches or kicks, but physical contact still carries a risk of injury. Train at a pace that suits your condition.

02 / LIFELONG

A Lifelong Sport for All Ages

You can adjust your effort as you train, regardless of age or build. Adults in their 20s through 50s and beyond can continue jiu-jitsu as a long-term hobby.

03 / WORLDWIDE

Human Chess (Intellectual Martial Art)

A cerebral martial art often called human chess. Because leverage and technical logic triumph over brute force, it has become deeply popular among professionals and entrepreneurs worldwide.

04 / WOMEN

A Welcoming Start for Women

Learn how to use your body without relying on strength alone. If you want to start exercising more, ask us about classes and training via the form or LINE.

05 / KIDS

Ideal for Kids’ Growth

Children learn proper etiquette and respect while naturally developing motor skills and confidence. Highly recommended for parents seeking quality youth activities in Honjo.

03 / Class Flow

Class Structure

03
STEP 01

Warm-up & Mat Drills

Practice essential movements like breakfalls (Ukemi) and hip escapes (Shrimping) to prevent injuries. Even complete beginners easily develop balance and body awareness.

STEP 02

Technique Practice

Watch detailed technique demonstrations by the instructor, then drill repeatedly in pairs. Senior members are always ready to guide you kindly.

STEP 03

Controlled Sparring (Rolling)

Put your techniques to the test in live practice (rolling). The joy comes from strategy and timing rather than brute force. Partners carefully match your level.

08 / FAQ

FAQ

Q1Is it OK to join if I am a complete beginner with no experience in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu or martial arts?

Yes, of course. Many people at our dojo started with no experience. Jiu-jitsu involves no strikes, giving it a high level of safety, and you can enjoy learning from the basics.

Q2Is it too late to start in my 30s or 40s to get more exercise or lose weight?

Not at all. Many adults in their 20s through 50s attend our dojo as a regular hobby. It is a full-body activity, so we also recommend it for weight management and stress relief.

Q3Can children and elementary school students learn manners and develop coordination through these classes?

Yes. In our kids classes, we teach not only jiu-jitsu techniques but also the importance of greetings and good manners. Mat exercises naturally help children develop the basic fitness that supports all kinds of sports.

Q4Does starting jiu-jitsu involve a large initial expense, such as buying a gi?

A gi is normally required, but you can attend a trial in comfortable clothing that is easy to move in. When you join, we will clearly explain when to buy a gi and how to choose one.

Q5Can women participate safely? Is jiu-jitsu useful for self-defense?

Yes, women can also participate with confidence. Jiu-jitsu focuses on controlling an opponent through leverage and body mechanics rather than strength alone, so we recommend it for self-defense as well.

Q6Can I drive to the dojo from outside Honjo? Is parking available?

Yes, we provide free parking. Our location is convenient for people in Honjo and nearby areas such as Kamisato, Kodama, Kamikawa, Misato, Fukaya, and Isesaki in Gunma Prefecture.

Q7Can I really come empty-handed for a visit or free trial?

Yes, please feel free to come for a visit or free trial. For a free trial, bringing a comfortable T-shirt and shorts, a towel, and a drink will help you be prepared.
